SaveMateKSA is a Telegram shopping assistant for Arabic-speaking users. Send a product name in Arabic or English, or a product link from Amazon or AliExpress, to see available product information and compare relevant offers. Replies are in Arabic.

## Try it

1. Open [@savemateksabot](https://t.me/savemateksabot) and press **Start**.
2. Join the [SaveMateKSA channel](https://t.me/savemateksa) when prompted, then verify your membership in the bot. Channel membership is required to use the service.
3. Send a product name such as `wireless mouse`, or paste a product link from Amazon or AliExpress.
4. Read the available details and follow an offer link to check the selected variant and total at checkout.

The bot is free to use. Some purchase links are affiliate links, and SaveMateKSA may earn a commission from qualifying purchases.

## What it does

- Searches Amazon and AliExpress without giving either marketplace priority.
- Accepts names in Arabic or English. If an Arabic search finds no suitable offers, it may translate the query and retry.
- Shows available product information, offer links and product image previews when available.
- Ranks offers by relevance, then by product price where the available currency and data allow a comparison.
- Labels similar alternatives when the same product cannot be confirmed.
- Provides experimental image search. Its accuracy and response time can vary.

## Before buying

The bot cannot guarantee a match or the lowest final price. Similar offers may differ in model, size, color, quantity or included accessories. Results depend on the data returned by the marketplaces.

Listed prices may exclude shipping, taxes or other charges. Check specifications, delivery to your address, availability and the final total on the store page before paying.

## Privacy and support

Use `/privacy` inside the bot for its current data-handling notice and `/forget` to delete your saved bot data. Send support questions to [@savemateksa2](https://t.me/savemateksa2).
